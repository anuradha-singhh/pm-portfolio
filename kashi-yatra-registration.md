# Kashi Yatra — Event Registration Volunteer

**Event:** Kashi Yatra, BHU cultural event
**Role:** Registration volunteer

## What I did
- Managed sign-ups and registration for around 200 participants
- Handled ID verification during a last-minute rush of entries close to the event
- Worked under time pressure to keep registration moving smoothly without slowing down the event

## What I learned
- How to stay organized when volume suddenly increases
- The importance of having a clear, fast verification process before an event starts
- Coordinating with a team under pressure while still being accurate
